# Sounds the port adds

The game's own sounds are the exe's (the banks the race loads). Everything here is the port's, laid over the race's mix, and **only in Restomod '26**: each one is behind a flag that is 0 in Stock and Restored, so those two play exactly the exe's sounds. Every sample is CC0 or public domain, with its source, author, licence and check date in [`data/sounds/LICENSES.md`](../data/sounds/LICENSES.md); the test of each sound checks that its entry is there. The flags are in [settings.md](settings.md).

All are 16-bit mono WAVs at the mixer's rate (22050 Hz) in `data/sounds/`, read once when first wanted. A missing or unusable file leaves that sound silent and says so once in the log (`wet_roll: no sounds/<file>: the roll is silent`); nothing else breaks.

## Thunder

- **When:** `weather=storm` with `rain_v2`. A clap follows each lightning 0.5 to 4 s later (`weather_thunder_delay`, a pure function of the tick, so a replay sounds the same), at full level for the shortest delay and 35 % for the longest (`weather_thunder_volume`). Inside a closed zone (a tunnel, a gallery) the clap is muffled to 40 % (`weather_thunder_inside`, by the rain's openness).
- **Not when:** no storm, `rain_v2` off, or the snow (Alpine 3 with `snow_weather`: the storm is snow, with no lightning and no thunder).
- **Where in the code:** `storm_thunder_update` (ported_view.c) strikes it, `drive_audio_thunder` (drive_audio.c) mixes it, `game/audio/thunder.c` reads the WAV (the loader the loops and the splashes below reuse).

## Rain

- **When:** `weather=rain` or `storm` with `rain_v2` and `rain_tyre_roll` (on in Restomod '26, off in Stock and Restored; the switch is read every frame, so a change of mode or of the flag is heard at once). What the eye hears (`rain_roll.h`), each a looping voice:
  - stopped, a view outside: the rain round the car (`rain_stopped_out.wav`) at 45 %; moving, the rain round a moving car (`rain_moving_out.wav`) at 68 % (the same loudness), played once whatever cars there are. From 2 to 15 m/s the first gives way to the second with an equal-power cross-fade;
  - stopped, the dash view: the rain on the roof (`rain_stopped_in.wav`) at 40 %; moving, the rain on the roof and the windscreen with the hiss of the car's own tyres (`rain_moving_in.wav`), cross-faded the same way, then rising from 111 % at 15 m/s to 221 % at 60 m/s (the level the old wet-road loop had in the cockpit). The car's own spray is not played in the cockpit: it is in this loop.
- **Every car's spray** (`rain_spray.wav`, `rain_cars.h`): the player's, the rivals' and the traffic's, each a source where the car is, panned by where it is from the eye as the exe pans the cars' sounds. Its level by the car's pace (a traffic car by how far it really goes, as its drawn spray: `spray_pace.h`): nothing below 2 m/s, the equal-power rise to 260 % at 15 m/s and 519 % at 60 m/s (the old wet road's loudness; the voice takes up to 600 %); pitch 800 permille of the sample's rate standing, its own rate at 25 m/s, at most 1100 at 60 m/s. By where it is heard from: 0 dB up to 4.35 m (the near chase camera), -6 dB at 6.8 m (the far one), then -6 dB each time the distance doubles, faded out from 40 to 60 m; 3 dB lower in the dash view; the followed car's own glides over a quarter of a second on a change of view. On the rally's gravel a quarter of that (-12 dB), a layer under the exe's gravel roll; none on a shoulder, the grass or in the air; none inside a closed zone at the car's own node (a car in a tunnel is silent, fading at the mouths). Each voice taken by a car starts at a random place in the loop, so the cars do not sound in phase.
- **Puddle splashes** (`rain_puddle_<n>.wav`, every file from 1 up to the first missing, at most 8): each car on asphalt throws them at random, none below 10 m/s, rising straight to about one a second at 60 m/s; each at a random gain of -6 to 0 dB and a random rate of ±5 %, never the same clip twice in a row, where the car is, at 100 % near the car and by the distance and view as its spray. The dice are the sound's own (no `race_rand`: the race and its replays are unchanged).
- **Voices:** the four loudest cars (`RAIN_CARS_VOICES`) are heard; a car keeps its voice while it is among the five loudest, and a voice its car lost rests 0.2 s (its fade) before another car takes it; at most six splashes at once. Measured: all of them playing cost 0.33 % of one core on the sound's thread (110 µs a frame), and a headless drive with eight rivals in rain runs as fast with them as without (11.7-12.6 s for 900 frames either way).
- **The mix:** the rain's loops and splashes sum into a 32-bit bus of their own, which meets the race's mix once (`rain_bus.h`). A full-throttle engine already takes the race's mix to full scale and a fast car's spray is over it on its own, so where the rain sounds the sum is rounded softly above 3/4 of full scale (a tanh curve that reaches full scale only at infinity) instead of clipping flat; below that, and wherever the bus is silent, it is the plain sum, so the race's own sound is untouched. Measured in 30 s at full throttle: 5,300-7,650 samples clipped flat with a plain sum, 0-1 rounded; the rain no quieter; 4-8 % of the samples touched. The bus has two inputs: the rain round a stopped car (`rain_stopped_out`, `rain_stopped_in`) as it is, and the moving rain (`rain_moving_out`, `rain_moving_in`, every car's `rain_spray`, the `rain_puddle_*` splashes) at half, -6 dB, by one gain where it enters the bus; the files and their levels above are as they were.
- **Pause and menu:** the pause and the in-race menu hush the bus: the rain is silent within 20 ms (a fade, no click), as the engine's voices go quiet there, and what its voices held is dropped. It wakes when the race's clock moves again and eases back from silence with the frame's own levels, so a mode or flag changed in the menu is what is heard on the way back (Stock, Restored, or the flag off: nothing). Leaving the race from the menu leaves it hushed.
- **Snow falling** (`snow_weather`): snow is silent, so no rain layer; it melts on the road, so every car's spray and splashes play as in rain, in the dash view too (3 dB lower).
- **Not when:** dry or fog; the snowy rally's track (`RALLY_GRAVEL_SNOW`: snow, not water; its own roll below); inside a closed zone (the openness at the eye silences the rain's layers, the openness at each car its spray).
- **Where in the code:** `rain_roll.[ch]` (the levels), `rain_cars.[ch]` (the splashes' dice and the voices), `weather_rain.c` (`weather_hooks_rain_roll`, once a frame), `drive_audio_rain_roll`, `drive_audio_rain_spray`, `drive_audio_rain_puddle` (drive_audio.c), `game/audio/wet_roll.[ch]` (the looping voice, with its pan), `game/audio/splash.[ch]` (the one-shot splashes) and `game/audio/rain_bus.[ch]` (where they meet the race's mix). The log says when a spray voice changes car (`rain: tick <t> spray voice <v> car <c>`).

## Roll on snow

- **When:** the rally on a snowy track only (`RALLY_GRAVEL_SNOW`: Alpine 3's places file says `snow 1`, with `snow_weather`); the followed car on the ground and moving. A drag over snow that sounds like a friction rising with speed: level 0 at 2 m/s to 90 % at 50 m/s, pitch 700 to 1600 permille, steeper than the spray's (`snow_roll.h`). In the dash view it is 6 dB lower; where snow falls too, the openness scales it as it does the rain.
- **Not when:** standing (under 2 m/s), in the air, inside a closed zone where snow falls, any other rally or track (snow falling there is the rain's spray). On the snow's rally the exe's gravel sample is no longer struck (race_sound.c), so this is the only roll heard there; the rain's layers are not heard on it.
- **Where in the code:** `snow_roll.[ch]` (the curves), `weather_hooks_snow_roll` (weather_hooks.c, once a frame), `drive_audio_snow_roll` (drive_audio.c, the same looping voice with its own sample).

## The loop voice

The loops are one mechanism (`game/audio/wet_roll.c`): a looping sample, its level, pitch and pan set by the caller each frame and eased so a change is no click. A caller that stops (the race's end) leaves it to fall silent after half a second; the pause and the in-race menu hush the rain's bus at once (above). It is mixed over the race's mix, in the device's thread and in the offline dump (`TNFS_AUDIO_DUMP=file.wav`, for checking a sound without a device: run the same drive with and without the sample file and compare the two dumps).

## Adding a sound

1. A CC0 or public-domain recording only; read the licence on its page, and say on which day.
2. Trim and loop it (a loop's end cross-faded into its start), down-mix to mono, 22050 Hz, about -18 dBFS RMS with the peak at half scale, into `data/sounds/<name>.wav`.
3. An entry in `LICENSES.md` with *What*, *Made from*, *Source*, *Author*, *Licence* and *Checked on the page on*, in the form of the entries there.
4. Behind a flag that is 0 in Stock and Restored (or behind a kind that already is, as the snow's is); pure functions for its level and pitch, with a test for the curve and for the licence entry (see `tests/test_rain_roll.c`, `tests/test_snow_roll.c`).
