**Spirited Drive** 0.9.2: *The Need for Speed* Special Edition (1996), rebuilt from scratch
to run natively on today's computers. Now on Windows as well as macOS. This release also brings the 1996 look back in Stock and Restored, and much better light in Restomod: sun shadows, softer tunnels and touched-up car lights and shine.

## Install

**macOS** (11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel)
1. Download `Spirited-Drive-0.9.2-macos.dmg`, open it and drag **Spirited Drive** to Applications.
2. The first time, macOS will say it cannot check the app. Right-click the app, **Open**, then
   **Open** again; or System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.

**Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit**
1. Download `Spirited-Drive-0.9.2-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run **Spirited Drive**.
2. The first time, Windows will warn that the app is from an unknown publisher: **More info**,
   then **Run anyway**.

On first start, point the game at your CD, folder or ISO. It copies what it needs. You need your own copy of the game: the PC Special Edition CD, a folder with its files, or an ISO image of your own disc. Spirited Drive contains no EA content.

**Updating?** On macOS, replace the old app. On Windows, delete the old Spirited Drive folder and unzip the new one in its place; do not unzip over it, because files a version removed would stay. Your profile, settings, records, mods and copied game files are kept (on Windows they are in `%APPDATA%\Spirited Drive`, which is not touched).

## New in 0.9.2

- **Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit)**: unzip it and play. There are no network races on Windows yet: Multiplayer is dimmed and says why.
- **Choose how to play**: the first start asks for Stock '96, Restored '96 or Restomod '26 and says what each one changes; change it any time on the Game Mode screen. The picture fills the screen by default, and Alt+Enter (Windows) or Cmd+Ctrl+F (macOS) switches full screen.
- **Stock '96 and Restored '96 look like 1996 again**: the black band under the outside view, movies in 4:3, and the things we added only in Restomod.
- **Light** (Restomod): the sun casts shadows at dawn, by day and at dusk; tunnels fade in and out and your headlights light the dark ones; reflectors and headlight halos work at dawn and dusk too; several cars' detail masks for lights and shine have been adjusted.
- **Cars** (Restomod): Torch Red for the Corvette ZR-1, paint that takes the whole body (911 Carrera, 512TR), and records per car: the showcase's RECORDS tile shows each car's best times and top speed on every track.
- **Mouse and pads**: a quick touchpad tap counts as a click, and a pad plugged in or pulled out mid-game is picked up.
- **Fixes**: no more absurd average speed after a short circuit race (884 mph), menu text without hard edges, and a network screen that leaves cleanly.

## Always in the workshop

Restomod's car shine and colours and its track lighting are being tuned track by track and car
by car. Expect some reflections in the wrong place, paint that does not take its colour, and
tunnels or shadows that look off. The engine is solid; this is art and tuning, and it gets
better with every update. Stock '96 and Restored '96 are not affected.

## Known issues

- Neither app is signed: macOS and Windows ask you to allow it the first time (see Install).
- Network races work in all three modes, on macOS only for now: the Windows version has none
  yet. They are very beta, little tested so far.
- Linux is planned, later.
