# Code of conduct

TNFS Restomod is a small hobby project made with care. Everyone who takes part — in issues, discussions, pull
requests or anywhere we gather — is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including with newcomers and people whose first language is not English;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- keep discussions on topic and assume good faith;
- respect the original game's creators and owners: no piracy, no links to copies of the game.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, doxxing, spam, or pressure to give anyone
copies of the game.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem privately,
use GitHub's "Report content" on the post, or contact the maintainers through the Bytes & Rust organisation page.
