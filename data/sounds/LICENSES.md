## rain_tyre_roll.wav

- What: the wet road under the tyres of a moving car in rain or falling snow (`rain_tyre_roll`, with `rain_v2`), a seamless loop, mono, 16-bit, 22050 Hz, 10.15 s. Its level and pitch rise with speed; it is 6 dB lower in the cockpit and lower still under a rally's gravel roll.
- Made from: "Graduation tower Busko Zdroj" (brine water spraying; the page's `-lq.mp3` preview, https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/699/699278_6746039-lq.mp3), down-mixed to mono at 22050 Hz: three continuous stretches, seconds 3.70 to 7.35, 16.30 to 19.95 and 28.90 to 32.55, joined with 0.25 s equal-power cross-fades; a high-pass at 150 Hz; looped with a 0.3 s equal-power cross-fade of the end into the start; the level set to -18 dBFS RMS with the peak at half scale. Every 50 ms of it is between -24.6 and -18.7 dBFS: no gaps. Nothing else changed.
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/DreamArtSoundDesigners/sounds/699278/
- Author: DreamArtSoundDesigners (https://freesound.org/people/DreamArtSoundDesigners/), uploaded 2023-08-14

## snow_roll.wav

- What: the roll of a car on the snowy rally's track (Alpine 3), a seamless loop, mono, 16-bit, 22050 Hz, 5.3 s. Silent when the car stands; its level and pitch rise steeply with speed, like a friction; 6 dB lower in the cockpit.
- Made from: "sled inuit drag across snow.wav" (the page's preview), down-mixed to mono at 22050 Hz: the four drag stretches, seconds 2.45 to 4.30, 5.20 to 6.80, 0.20 to 1.60 and 7.70 to 9.05, joined in that order with 0.2 s cross-fades; a high-pass at 180 Hz and a low-pass at 9 kHz; looped with a 0.3 s equal-power cross-fade of the end into the start; the level set to -18 dBFS RMS with the peak at half scale. Nothing else changed.
